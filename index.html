Who We Are

A Qatari technology partner built for what's next

Qayat Technologies is a new software and AI research company based in Doha. Our engineers, data scientists and researchers help organisations across Qatar modernise how they work, launch digital products and adopt artificial intelligence safely.

Qayat AI Lab

Specialised in AI research

Our in-house lab runs applied research and puts the results into production. We focus on challenges that matter to Qatar and the Arabic-speaking world.

🗣️ Arabic NLP & Large Language Models

Fine-tuning and evaluating LLMs for Arabic and Gulf dialects, bilingual chatbots, document understanding and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

🤖 Generative AI & AI Agents

Enterprise copilots, knowledge assistants and agentic workflows that connect securely to your internal systems.

👁️ Computer Vision

Object detection, OCR for Arabic documents, video analytics and visual inspection for smart cities and industry.

📈 Predictive Analytics & ML

Forecasting, anomaly detection, recommendation systems and optimisation models built on your data.

⚡ AI for Energy & Sustainability

Predictive maintenance, energy optimisation and environmental modelling for Qatar's energy and infrastructure sectors.

⚖️ Responsible & Trustworthy AI

Explainability, fairness, privacy-preserving ML and AI governance frameworks you can audit.

We collaborate with universities, research institutes and enterprises on joint research, proofs of concept and AI readiness assessments.
